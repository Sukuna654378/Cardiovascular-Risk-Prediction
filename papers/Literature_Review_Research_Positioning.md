## 1. Academic Search Strategy & Discovery Tools
A systematic literature search was conducted across **Google Scholar**, **Semantic Scholar**, **PubMed Central (PMC)**, **arXiv**, **medRxiv**, and **IEEE Xplore** using multi-keyword search strings:
- **Concept 1 (Target Condition):** `"Cardiovascular Disease"` OR `"Coronary Heart Disease"` OR `"CHD Risk"` OR `"Cardiovascular Event Prediction"`
- **Concept 2 (Data / Cohort):** `"Framingham Heart Study"` OR `"Framingham Cohort"` OR `"Imbalanced Medical Datasets"`
- **Concept 3 (Methodology / ML):** `"Data Leakage"` OR `"Cost-Sensitive Learning"` OR `"Threshold Optimization"` OR `"Precision-Recall AUC"` OR `"Decision Curve Analysis"`

### Discovery Tools & Snowballing Methodology
1. **Citation Snowballing (Forward & Backward):** Applied via Google Scholar "Cited by" on foundational risk models (\cite{basu_regulation_2009}, \cite{weng_can_2017}) to identify recent 2025–2026 benchmarks.
2. **Co-Citation Mapping:** Utilized **Connected Papers** and **ResearchRabbit** to map relationships between algorithmic frameworks (\cite{chen_xgboost_2016}) and clinical utility evaluation metrics (\cite{vickers_decision_2006}).
3. **Open Access Filtering:** Verified full-text availability across PMC, PLOS ONE, BMJ, arXiv, and medRxiv repositories.
4. **Benchmark Verification:** Cross-referenced dataset schemas and model rankings via **Papers with Code**.

## 4. Research Positioning Statement
> "Existing works train standard machine learning models using basic mean/median imputation, naive accuracy evaluation, unengineered raw features, and default 0.50 classification decision thresholds. However, no prior study has combined strict leak-free k-NN cross-validation with clinical risk interaction feature engineering (Pulse Pressure, Mean Arterial Pressure, Atherogenic Index), cost-sensitive loss weighting, and Precision-Recall decision threshold tuning on the 3,390-patient Framingham 10-year cardiovascular risk dataset. We deliver a leak-free Machine Learning framework that eliminates data leakage, resolves the accuracy paradox under heavy class imbalance, and optimizes decision thresholds specifically for clinical recall and sex-stratified net benefit."

### Formula Breakdown:
- **X (Current Literature):** Train standard ML algorithms using naive global imputation, accuracy evaluation under imbalance, raw unengineered features, and default 0.50 decision thresholds.
- **Y (Identified Gap / Innovation):** Encapsulate $k$-NN imputation strictly within CV folds, engineer non-linear clinical ratio features (PP, MAP, Atherogenic Index), apply cost-sensitive weighting ($\text{weight} \approx 5.63$), and optimize probability decision thresholds directly along the Precision-Recall curve.
- **Z (Clinical Context):** 10-year Coronary Heart Disease (CHD) risk prediction on the 3,390-patient Framingham cohort using routine non-invasive markers.
- **Our Proposal:** Deliver a leak-free ML framework that eliminates data leakage, handles $15.1\%$ class imbalance, and maximizes clinical sensitivity (Recall) and sex-stratified net benefit across actionable intervention thresholds ($10\%$--$30\%$).
