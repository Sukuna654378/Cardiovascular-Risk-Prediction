# AI Assistance & Research Tool Usage Declaration

## 1. Literature Search & Mapping Methodology
In accordance with course academic integrity guidelines, literature discovery and verification were conducted using:
* **Google Scholar & Semantic Scholar:** Executed forward ("Cited by") and backward reference snowballing on foundational risk papers (`D'Agostino et al., 2008`, `Weng et al., 2017`).
* **Connected Papers & ResearchRabbit:** Generated co-citation graph maps connecting decision curve analysis (`Vickers & Elkin, 2006`) with gradient boosted tree benchmarks (`Chen & Guestrin, 2016`).
* **PubMed Central (PMC) & Europe PMC:** Filtered and retrieved 100% legal, paywall-free full-text articles (PMCIDs, PLOS ONE, BMJ, medRxiv, arXiv, MDPI, Patterns).
* **Papers with Code:** Verified dataset schemas and benchmark rankings on the public Framingham Heart Study dataset.

## 2. AI Generative Tool Usage
* **Tools Used:** Gemini / ChatGPT
* **Scope of Usage:**
  1. Parsing and mapping Zotero BibTeX exports into the structured 20-paper literature matrix.
  2. Resolving BibTeX key citation mappings for Overleaf LaTeX compilation.
  3. Synthesizing academic English prose and positioning formulas.
* **Human Validation:** All 20 references were verified for existence, active working URLs/PMCIDs, and content accuracy by team members Naoual Houssni and Ilyass El Bakkali.
